## What a Deployment is

**One Broker, together with every room and every channel served through it.** A
Broker is one message-broker endpoint — one host and port that the agent
extension and the room client connect to. It is identified by that **endpoint**,
not by the machine, container, VM or OS instance the software happens to run on,
and not by how many agents, seats, users or rooms hang off it. Installing or
re-installing tabichat on more machines does not add a Deployment unless those
machines are reached through a different Broker endpoint.

**Non-production does not count:** development, test, demonstration and training
Deployments are free of charge, and a redundant or fail-over Broker kept up to
serve one production Broker counts as that same one Deployment.

Nothing on this page prices or licenses any third-party message-broker software.
The unit is a Deployment of tabichat, described by the endpoint its users connect
to.
